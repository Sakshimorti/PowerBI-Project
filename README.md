# **YASH ECOMMERCE SALES DASHBOARD**

## **Project Description**
This Power BI dashboard visualizes the **sales performance** of an e-commerce company named *Madhav* across various dimensions such as state, customer, category, payment mode, and time (months and quarters). It provides key performance indicators (KPIs) and insightful visualizations to support data-driven decision-making.

## **Key Metrics and KPIs**
- **Total Sales Amount:** ₹2M  
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 2505 units  
- **Average Order Value (AVO):** ₹257K  
- **Total Profit Earned:** ₹191K  

## **Visualizations**
- **Sum of Amount by State:**  
  A horizontal bar chart shows top-performing states like Karnataka, Jharkhand, Meghalaya, and Nagaland.

- **Sum of Qty by Category:**  
  A donut chart shows the percentage distribution of quantity sold across different product categories.

- **Sum of Profit by Month:**  
  A vertical bar chart displays monthly profit trends from January to December.

- **Sum of Amount by Customer Name:**  
  A bar chart shows top customers based on the amount spent, including Suraj, Komal, Akshata, Rutuja, and Omkar.

- **Sum of Qty by Payment Mode:**  
  A donut chart shows the breakdown of payment methods used (COD, CC, DC, EMI).

- **Sum of Profit by Sub-Category:**  
  A bar chart presents profitability across sub-categories such as Bookcases, Sofa, Table, and Tiles.

## **Filters and Interactivity**
- **Quarter Filter:** Allows filtering data by Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.  
- **State Filter:** Enables analysis based on selected states like Andhra Pradesh.  
- All visuals are interactive and support dynamic filtering.

## **Aesthetic Features**
- Dark-themed red background image with 27% transparency for a modern and elegant look.  
- Clear layout with well-aligned charts and KPIs for better readability.

## **Data Sources**
The dashboard uses data from two sheets:  
- **Sheet1:** Includes City, Customer Name, Order Date, Order ID, and State.  
- **Sheet2:** Includes Amount, AVO, Category, Order ID, Payment Mode, Profit, Qty, and Sub-Category.

## **Purpose**
This dashboard provides business stakeholders with a comprehensive overview of **sales and profit performance**, helping to identify trends, top-performing areas, and opportunities for strategic improvements.
